Non-negotiable rules from the brief

Hard constraints
01
Minimum display

Every store retains minimum display stock per category.

02
Store capacity

Never overfill a store beyond its capacity.

03
Flagship protection

S++ stores never give away their protected top-20 styles.

04
Transit awareness

Stock already in transit counts as arriving; do not send it twice.

05
30-day source cover

No store-to-store move may leave the giving store below 30 days of cover.

06
Lead times

Ignore transport cost, but account for delivery lead time.
